# FlockLocations.com

A community-powered map and open database of Flock Safety and ALPR (automated license plate reader) camera locations.

- Website: https://flocklocations.com
- Methodology: https://flocklocations.com/methodology
- Open dataset (CC BY 4.0):
  - Hugging Face: https://huggingface.co/datasets/FlockLocations/flocklocations-camera-map
  - Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/flocklocations/flocklocations-camera-map
  - Zenodo: https://zenodo.org/records/23318982
  - CSV export: https://flocklocations.com/api/cameras/export?format=csv

Camera records are labeled "Community Confirmed" (added or confirmed by community members) or "3rd Party / Unconfirmed" (aggregated from third-party sources, awaiting community confirmation).

## Contributing

Report a camera or confirm a sighting on the map at https://flocklocations.com - no account needed. Data questions: press@flocklocations.com
